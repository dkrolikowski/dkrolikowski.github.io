Welcome! I'm Danny Krolikowski, a postdoc at Steward Observatory and a member of the [NEID](https://neid.psu.edu) and [HPF](https://hpf.psu.edu) instrument teams, two cutting edge extreme precision radial velocity (EPRV) spectrometers.

Most of my time is spent working with the NEID software team to expand the functionality of its [data reduction pipeline](https://neid.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/NEID-DRP/) and support NEID's users! I also have been involved in EPRV community-wide efforts on data and software standardization, including as the PI of a NASA-funded effort to develop the infrastructure of a community data reduction framework for EPRV spectroscopy.

I received my Ph.D. from UT Austin in August of 2022, where I worked with [Professor Adam Kraus](https://astronomy.utexas.edu/component/cobalt/item/11-astronomy/1273-kraus-adam?Itemid=1272) on a variety of projects regarding young stars and their planetary systems.

In particular, I'm interested in understanding how stars and planets form and evolve in their early lifetimes. I have worked on projects regarding young stellar populations, stellar activity, exoplanet detection and characterization, and the handling of spectroscopic data.
